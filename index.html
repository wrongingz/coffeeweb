STAGES OF COFFEE HARVESTING

  1. Planting – Coffee seeds or seedlings are planted in suitable soil.
  2. Harvesting – Ripe coffee cherries are picked from the coffee plants.
  3. Processing – The cherries are pulped, fermented, washed, and dried to remove the outer layer
  4. Packaging – The roasted beans or ground coffee are packed into bags or containers ready for distribution and sale.
